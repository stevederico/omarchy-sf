Omarchy SF

Omarchy SF is the San Francisco Omarchy meetup. Bring your laptop and show what you've built with Omarchy: plugins, themes, workflows, agents and hardware. Or just come hang out and see how other people made Omarchy theirs.

Community-run meetup. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Omarchy or 37signals.
